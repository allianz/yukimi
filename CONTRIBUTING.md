# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Yukimi! We'd love to hear from you. 

Yukimi is still young, and our contributor documentation and process aren't in place yet. If you'd
like to get involved, drop us a line at
[christoph.held@allianz.de](mailto:christoph.held@allianz.de) — we're happy to talk about ideas,
use cases or ways to help.

Bug reports and feature ideas are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/allianz/yukimi/issues).
