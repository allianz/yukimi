# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Yukimi to be a project where people are treated with respect and can
participate regardless of who they are or where they come from. Contributors,
maintainers and anyone else taking part in this project are expected to act
professionally and in good faith.

## The standard we adopt

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]**. The
canonical text is authoritative, and we adopt it in full rather than keeping a
copy here that could drift out of date. Please read it:

- Full text: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
- Translations: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/>
- FAQ: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq/>

In short: contribute constructively, assume good faith in others, accept that
review is about the work rather than the person, and do not make this an
uncomfortable place for anyone to participate.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens — issues, pull requests, code
review, commit messages and discussions — and to anyone representing the project
in a public space.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers named in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), by direct message on GitHub or by email.

If your concern is **about** one of the maintainers, report it to the other one.
If it concerns all of them, or you would rather not go through the project at
all, an Allianz contributor can raise it through Allianz's internal compliance
channels instead.

> Note on channels: `SECURITY.md` routes *technical vulnerabilities* through
> GitHub's private vulnerability reporting. That mechanism is for security
> reports and is not an appropriate place for a Code of Conduct concern, so this
> file deliberately points somewhere else.

All reports are handled privately. We will acknowledge your report, tell you who
is handling it, and respect the reporter's privacy — we will not share your
identity beyond the people who need it to act.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for acting on reports and for explaining their
decisions to the people involved. Depending on severity, a response may range
from a private conversation, through editing or removing a contribution, to a
temporary or permanent block from the project.

The Contributor Covenant's [Enforcement Guidelines][guidelines] describe the
escalation ladder we follow.
